About Me

I am a dedicated bioinformatics scientist and biomedical data analyst connected to diverse computational biomedicine projects since 2007. I possess a wide skillset in biomedical machine learning, rational drug design, structural bioinformatics, computational genomics, and health data analytics. I have deep meticulousness, simultaneously tending to grasp the big picture. Proficient in leadership, prioritisation and communication. Looking to undertake a role in cutting-edge biomedical AI and health data analysis in a leading organisation.
